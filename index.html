About

I'm a Grade 12 student from Brampton heading to Computer Engineering at Waterloo this fall. What drives me is a compulsion to fix problems I can't ignore, whether that's a student who can't get proper mentorship, a community without health resources in their language, or a spreadsheet I was filling in every Sunday morning.
